Verified Research Basis (Section 15)

Academic citations verified on 24 Oct 2026. Explicitly documenting what we cite and what we do not claim.

1. Haase, J. and Pokutta, S. (2024) Human-AI Co-Creativity: Exploring Synergies Across Levels of Creative Collaboration. arXiv:2411.12527.
What we take: The 4-level vocabulary (Digital Pen, AI Task Specialist, AI Assistant, AI Co-Creator). SousArtist operates strictly as an AI Assistant coaching human agency.
Do not claim: That generative AI inherently harms creative agency or joy.
2. de Waal, A. et al. (2025) Therapeutic Approaches for the Prevention of Upper Limb Repetitive Strain Injuries in Work-Related Computer Use. Journal of Occupational Rehabilitation 35(2):234-267.
What we take: 58 included studies on computer workstation interventions.
Do not claim: That shortcut learning or coaching reduces RSI; no clinical strain claims are made.
3. Alimin, S. R. et al. (2025) Touch and Gesture-Based Interfaces in Creative Workflows: A Review. Malaysian J ICT 10(2).
What we take: Documenting the rise of hybrid touch, stylus, and gesture inputs in creative workflows.
4. Dang, Q.-V. (2026) Artificial intelligence as assistive technology for disabled students in higher education. J Enabling Technol.
What we take: Acknowledges the legitimate argument that generation is assistive; our architectural response is helping at the input while preserving human ownership.

Honest Limitations & Roadmap

• Solid Fills Only: Contrast analysis inspects solid color text against solid ancestor containers. Gradients, background imagery, and blend modes are skipped.

• Learning Aid, Not Legal Audit: SousArtist is an educational coach to build muscle memory; it is not a certified accessibility compliance audit.

• Roadmap Claims: Voice/switch input, Krita adapters, and cohort analytics are future roadmap exploration items, not shipped MVP features.
